# 💡 Feature Ideas & Future Roadmap

A collection of feature ideas and enhancement concepts for the **Cricket Auction Engine**. You can reference this document anytime to plan future updates.

---

## 🎨 Category 1: Live Auction & Event Experience

### Idea 1: 📢 Live AI Voice Announcer (Text-to-Speech)
* **Description**: Automated stadium announcer using the browser's native SpeechSynthesis API.
* **Functionality**:
  * Announces live bids on the Projector screen: *"Current bid 50,000 by Goverdhan Haveli..."*
  * Final countdown & sold announcement: *"Going once... Going twice... SOLD to Goverdhan Haveli for 16 Lakhs!"*

---

### Idea 2: ⚡ Turbo Bid Multipliers (`+10k`, `+25k`, `+50k`, `+1 Lakh`)
* **Description**: Quick action increment buttons in the Live Auction admin panel.
* **Functionality**: Allows the admin to quickly increase bids during fast-paced bidding wars without manual typing.

---

### Idea 3: 🏆 Sponsor Milestone Popups on Live Projector Screen
* **Description**: Dynamic sponsor banner overlays during key auction moments.
* **Functionality**: Triggers a *"Powered by [Sponsor Name]"* visual banner when total squad bid crosses major milestones (e.g., ₹10 Lakhs).

---

### Idea 4: 📱 Team Owner Mobile Bidding Pad (QR Code Access)
* **Description**: Dedicated mobile view for team owners.
* **Functionality**: Team owners scan a unique QR code with their phone to view real-time purse stats and tap **"Raise Bid"** directly from their mobile screen.

---

### Idea 5: 🎥 OBS Studio Live Stream Overlay Widget
* **Description**: Transparent HTML/CSS browser source URL (`/obs-overlay`) designed specifically for YouTube / Facebook Live Stream broadcasts.
* **Functionality**: Displays active player card, current bid price, and leading team logo in a sleek lower-third overlay for video streams.

---

### Idea 6: 🃏 Right-to-Match (RTM) Cards & Player Trade Window
* **Description**: Official RTM retention cards and post-auction player trading.
* **Functionality**:
  * **RTM Card Button**: Allows a player's previous team to match the highest bid during live bidding and retain the player.
  * **Trade Window**: Post-auction portal for teams to swap players with purse adjustments.

---

## 👕 Category 2: T-Shirt & Logistics Operations

### Idea 7: 🎽 T-Shirt Logistics & Delivery Signature Tracker
* **Description**: End-to-end jersey distribution management.
* **Functionality**:
  * Status tracker: `Pending` → `Sent to Vendor` → `Received` → `Handed to Player`.
  * Printable Delivery Receiving Slip PDF for team owners/captains to sign upon receiving jerseys.

---

### Idea 8: 🎫 Printable Player Tournament ID Badges & Ground Passes (PDF)
* **Description**: Auto-generated tournament accreditation passes (Lanyard or Wallet size).
* **Functionality**: Includes Player Photo, Full Name, Role, Team Name & Logo, T-Shirt Jersey Number, and a QR Code for ground check-in on match days.

---

## 📊 Category 3: Analytics, Reports & Exports

### Idea 9: 📊 Auction Summary & Media Press Release Sheet (PDF)
* **Description**: 1-Click media recap sheet for WhatsApp groups, Instagram, and press.
* **Functionality**: Highlights Most Expensive Players (Top Batsman, Top Bowler, Top All-Rounder), Overall Purse Spent per Team, and Tournament Stats.

---

### Idea 10: 📊 Team Squad Composition & Role Warning Bar
* **Description**: Real-time position requirement indicators on `/team-details` and live bidding.
* **Functionality**: Shows position gauges (e.g. `Batsmen: 4/4 ✅`, `Bowlers: 2/4 ⚠️ Need 2 more`) so owners know what roles they still need during live bidding.

---

### Idea 11: 📅 Match Fixtures & Playing XI Roster Sheet Generator
* **Description**: Post-auction tournament fixture generator.
* **Functionality**: Automatically creates round-robin match schedules and printable Playing XI team sheets for captains before each match.

---

### Idea 12: 📜 Custom Tournament Rulebook & Policy PDF
* **Description**: Downloadable official tournament rulebook for team owners.
* **Functionality**: Exports rules on squad minimums, purse penalties, substitute rules, and RTM policies.

---

## 👥 Category 4: Registration & Player Management

### Idea 13: 📸 Live Webcam Photo Capture with Auto-Crop
* **Description**: Direct camera photo capture for player registration.
* **Functionality**: Allows players/admins to take photos directly using mobile/laptop webcams with instant circle/square cropping preview.

---

### Idea 14: 🔄 Second Chance Round for Unsold Players
* **Description**: Dedicated Round 2 auction pool.
* **Functionality**: Quickly filter and re-auction unsold players with a reduced base price or accelerated random draw.

---

### Idea 15: 📱 Bulk WhatsApp Broadcast Message Link Generator
* **Description**: Instant notification link builder.
* **Functionality**: Generates WhatsApp broadcast links to send tournament updates to registered players or team owners in one click.

---

## 💰 Category 5: Tournament Finance & Fan Engagement

### Idea 16: 💰 Team Owner Financial Receipts & Invoice PDF
* **Description**: Franchise fee financial tracking.
* **Functionality**: Track team owner entry payments, security deposits, and print official Payment Receipt PDFs.

---

### Idea 17: 🗳️ Public Fan Polls & "Strongest Squad" Voting
* **Description**: Interactive fan voting on the public homepage.
* **Functionality**: Allows fans to vote for which team drafted the strongest squad, rendering live percentage charts.

---

### Idea 18: 🏆 Digital Hall of Fame & Past Winners Archive
* **Description**: Historical archive of previous tournament seasons.
* **Functionality**: Showcase past winning teams, high-resolution trophy photos, and legendary player records.
